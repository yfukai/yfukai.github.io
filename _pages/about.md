I am Yohsuke T. Fukai, an associate professor in the Department of Physics, Faculty of Science, at Kyushu University (Fukuoka, Japan). I am interested in the universal and robust physics underlying biological and other nonequilibrium systems. In particular, I have studied topics related to cell differentiation and chromatin structure, as well as universal fluctuations in nonequilibrium interface growth. I am launching a laboratory at Kyushu University after spending several years as a postdoctoral researcher in the Nonequilibrium Physics of Living Matter Laboratory at RIKEN. I also have a passion for software development and am a keen contributor to open-source projects.
